Volunteer List Parser

Turn the Assigned Volunteers report into one row per volunteer, with each day's roles side by side.

🔒 Your file stays on this computer. Anything you download or print still contains volunteer PII.

How to use
  1. In VMS, open your event and click All Reports.
  2. Export Assigned Volunteers and drop it in the box.
  3. Check the preview, filter to key volunteers if you like, then click Download CSV.
