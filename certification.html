Certification Status Checker

Instructions:

  1. Log in to VMS and select your event.
  2. Click All Reports.
  3. Click Training and Certifications.
  4. Click Export.
  5. Click the button below to select your export.
All data processing happens on your computer and is never sent to a server. No worries about PII here! Emails sent with the tool below will be BCC'd by default.
Limitation: The Training and Certification Report only reports on trainings which are assigned already. Prior to certifications being set to live, volunteers will not appear on this list. Also, not all roles with required training have certification components and thus will not appear on this report.
