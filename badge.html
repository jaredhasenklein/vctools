Name Badge Generator

Instructions:

  1. Log in to VMS and select your event.
  2. Click All Reports, then Name Badges (or Assigned Volunteers), then Export.
  3. Drop the file in the box below.
  4. Pick your label stock (Avery 5163 sheets or Dymo 2x4 thermal labels) and a template.
  5. Optionally, fill in the event name and bottom corners. The preview shows each volunteer's badge.
  6. Optionally, click Customize to change the layout: move and resize text, change sizes and colors, and add your own text.
  7. Optionally, shorten long role names in the abbreviations table.
  8. Click Generate Labels to download the PDF.
All data processing happens on your computer and is never sent to a server. No worries about PII here! Just remember that the file you download from here may contain PII, just like the export from VMS.
