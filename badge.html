Name Badge Generator

Print a badge for every assigned volunteer on Avery 5163 sheets or Dymo 2x4 labels.

🔒 Your file stays on this computer. Anything you download or print still contains volunteer PII.

How to use
  1. In VMS, open your event and click All Reports.
  2. Export Name Badges (or Assigned Volunteers) and drop it in the box.
  3. Pick your label stock and a design. The preview shows each volunteer's badge.
  4. Optionally fill in the event name and bottom corners, shorten long roles, or click Edit this design / Start a blank badge to make your own.
  5. Click Generate Labels to download the PDF.

Design your badge

  1. Add what you want on the badge.
  2. Drag things on the preview to move them; drag the blue corner to resize.
  3. Click anything to change how it looks.
