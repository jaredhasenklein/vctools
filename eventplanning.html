Event Planning Reporter

Meal counts by dietary need, shirt sizes, years of service, and consent status for your event.

🔒 Your file stays on this computer. Anything you download or print still contains volunteer PII.

How to use
  1. In VMS, open your event and click All Reports.
  2. Export Attendance & Event Planning and drop it in the box.
  3. Adjust meal times if needed (defaults: breakfast 7:30am, lunch 12:00pm, dinner 6:00pm).
  4. Review the reports, or click Export All Data to Excel.
