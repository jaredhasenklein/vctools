Event Planning Reporter

Instructions:

  1. Log in to VMS and select your event.
  2. Click All Reports.
  3. Click Attendance & Event Planning.
  4. Click Export.
  5. Click or drag your file to the box on the right to select your export.
  6. Configure meal times if needed (defaults: breakfast 7:30am, lunch 12:00pm, dinner 6:00pm).
  7. View the generated stats reports.
