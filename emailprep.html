Volunteer Email Prep Beta

Combine three VMS reports into one spreadsheet for mail-merge reminder emails.

How to use
  1. In VMS, open your event and click All Reports.
  2. Export Assigned Volunteers, Training and Certifications, and Consent and Release Status, and drop each in its box below (any order).
  3. Click Generate Output to preview, then Download CSV.

🔒 Your files stay on this computer. Anything you download or print still contains volunteer PII.
