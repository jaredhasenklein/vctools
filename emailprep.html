Volunteer Email Prep

Upload the three VMS export files to generate a flattened volunteer status spreadsheet for email outreach.

Instructions

  1. Log in to VMS and select your event.
  2. Click All Reports.
  3. Export the Assigned Volunteers, Training and Certifications, and Consent and Release Status reports.
  4. Upload all three files below (order doesn't matter).
  5. Click Generate Output to preview, then Download CSV.

All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to a server.
