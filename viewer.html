Report Viewer

Open any VMS report as a table you can search, sort, trim to the columns you need, then download or print.

  1. In VMS, open your event, click All Reports, pick any report, and click Export.
  2. Drop the file below.

All processing happens on your computer and is never sent to a server. Downloads and printouts still contain volunteer PII, so handle them like the VMS export.
