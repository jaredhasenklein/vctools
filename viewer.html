Report Viewer

Open any VMS report as a table you can search, sort, and trim to the columns you need, then download or print.

🔒 Your file stays on this computer. Anything you download or print still contains volunteer PII.

How to use
  1. In VMS, open your event and click All Reports.
  2. Pick any report, click Export, and drop the file in the box.
  3. Search, click a column name to sort, or use Columns to hide what you don't need.
  4. Click Download CSV or Print.
